01

Battery lifetime

Find out how active bursts and sleep current affect an IoT device’s battery budget.

Example: a 2,000 mAh battery with 80% usable capacity, a 20 mA burst for 1 second every 60 seconds and 10 µA sleep current gives approximately 194 days.

How it works & model limitations

Average current = active current × duty cycle + sleep current × (1 − duty cycle). Lifetime = usable capacity ÷ average current.

Use battery-side currents, including regulator and peripheral consumption. This model assumes a repeating cycle and constant usable capacity. It does not predict self-discharge, pulse-related voltage sag, temperature effects or aging. The usable-capacity percentage is your assumption, not an automatic chemistry correction.

02

Camera motion blur

Estimate image smear and choose an exposure time for a moving subject.

Example: 200 mm across 2,000 pixels, at 1,000 mm/s and 100 µs exposure, gives 1 pixel of motion blur. A 2-pixel limit permits up to 200 µs.

How it works & model limitations

Scale = field of view ÷ image width. Blur = speed × exposure ÷ scale. Maximum exposure = allowed blur × scale ÷ speed.

Assumes constant lateral movement in the object plane. Use the actual field of view and output pixel width after ROI, resizing or binning. It does not model rolling-shutter distortion, vibration, defocus, diffraction or lighting. Zero speed has no motion-based exposure limit in this model.

03

Supercapacitor hold-up

Estimate how long a capacitor bank can support a constant-power load.

Example: 10 F from 5 V down to 3 V stores 80 J in the usable voltage window. At 80% efficiency and a 4 W load, estimated hold-up is 16 seconds.

How it works & model limitations

Stored energy = ½ × C × (start voltage² − minimum voltage²). Hold-up time = stored energy × efficiency ÷ load power.

Enter effective bank capacitance, not individual cell capacitance for a series bank. Efficiency is treated as constant. ESR voltage drop, converter current limits, startup, leakage, balancing losses, aging and temperature are not modeled. Set minimum voltage to reflect the actual converter and load requirements. Validate with the component datasheets and a measured discharge test.
