How to turn meeting notes into action items with AI

Which deadline did someone accept?

A Monday release meeting contains three dates or timing cues. Identify what the team can put in its action register.

LineSpeakerWords
L1AlexI will share two drafts by Wednesday.
L2BeaCould we ship on Friday?
L3ChairWe will decide the release date after QA.
Choose the action supported by the transcript.

Read the worked answer

L1 gives an owner, a deliverable and a deadline. L2 proposes a date. L3 defers the decision and does not assign QA to the chair.

Try this prompt on the numbered transcript: “List each commitment and quote its source line. Put proposals in a separate list and ask a question wherever ownership is missing.” Check each owner and date against the actual words.
