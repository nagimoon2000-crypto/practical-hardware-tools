Which workflow do you need?

  • CCTV: estimate retention and document recording assumptions
  • I2C: review pullup limits and scope measurements
  • Battery: record operating states and compare current
  • Edge AI: compare workloads, power and cooling

Battery budget: use operating-state current and duration to estimate average battery current and runtime. A short transmit burst and a long sleep interval must both be represented.

Edge AI validation: record power, temperatures and completed outputs under a defined workload. Compare cooling or model settings only after fixing the measurement boundary.

A calculator gives an estimate. A workbook helps keep inputs, measurements and assumptions together. Neither is a substitute for testing your device.

Why does a rail-to-rail op-amp still clip?

Review the output limits at the actual load, supply and temperature. In a synthetic 0–5 V example, 0.1 V low-rail and 0.2 V high-rail headroom give limits of 0.1–4.8 V. A 2.5 V center and 2 V peak request 0.5–4.5 V, leaving 0.4 V and 0.3 V of voltage margin.

Check the return node: a 10 kohm load to ground demands 0.05–0.45 mA sourcing. Returned to 2.5 V, it instead demands −0.2 mA sinking to +0.2 mA sourcing. Recheck whether the same headroom bounds apply; this is not a guaranteed device example.

IoT battery life and current budget spreadsheet

Useful for: battery-powered sensors, periodic telemetry and sleep/wake devices with a repeating operating cycle.

What the paid workbook includes

  • 12 operating-state rows, weighted average current and runtime in hours and days.
  • 20 rows to compare estimated and measured current, including mA and percentage differences.
  • Method guidance, units, measurement boundaries and an illustrative example.

A practical first session

  1. List every non-overlapping state in a complete cycle: waking, measuring, transmitting and sleeping.
  2. Record battery-terminal current and time in each state. Do not add downstream rail currents without accounting for conversion.
  3. Calculate the weighted mean, then compare it with a current measurement over a complete cycle.
  4. Investigate the largest mismatch before changing the battery capacity assumption.

Free starter: three states and the method guide; no measurement log. Select Preview on the product page to inspect it.

Limits: the simple model excludes self-discharge, calendar aging, pulse-voltage drop and nonlinear battery behavior. It supplies no measured device lifetime. Generation-engine checks are not native Excel recalculation or hardware validation.

Edge AI power and thermal test spreadsheet

Useful for: comparing model configurations and cooling setups on low-voltage development boards operated within manufacturer ratings.

What the paid workbook includes

  • Eight workload/cooling scenarios and eight symptom-isolation comparisons.

A practical first session

  1. Record the board, model, runtime, input shape, cooling and ambient conditions.

Before choosing a workbook

Can I start without buying?

Yes. The browser calculators work without a purchase. Use the Preview link on Payhip to inspect the available sample. Preview formats vary: the CCTV pack provides a text preview; the battery and Edge AI packs provide starter spreadsheets. A preview is not the complete paid workbook.

Are these real hardware test results?

No. These are independent planning and recording templates. Examples illustrate a method and do not establish the performance of your device.

Will the workbook run in my spreadsheet application?

The products use editable XLSX files and ordinary Excel formulas. Inspect the starter in your own application before purchasing. Native Microsoft Excel recalculation has not been validated for these packs.
