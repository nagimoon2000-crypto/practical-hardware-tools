AI camera storage

Plan recording capacity from the measured encoded bitrate of your camera streams.

Example: four 4 Mbps cameras recording 24 hours/day for 7 days produce 1.2096 TB of video; adding 20% overhead gives 1.45152 TB.

How it works & model limitations

Daily GB = cameras × Mbps × 0.45 × recording hours. Required TB = daily GB × retention days × (1 + overhead / 100) ÷ 1000. GB and TB use decimal units.
