How much does an oscilloscope probe load a high-impedance node?

A 10 kohm source with a 10 Mohm, 10 pF probe retains 99.9001% of its open-circuit DC amplitude. The parallel resistance is 9.9900 kohm, giving an added pole near 1.59314 MHz. Use Rp/(Rs+Rp) for amplitude and 1/(2 pi (Rs parallel Rp) Cp) for the pole.

What this does not establish

A small DC error does not establish good edge fidelity. This pole describes probe loading, not total instrument bandwidth. Probe inductance and frequency-dependent source impedance need a fuller model.

Review check

Record probe mode, tip capacitance and source impedance; compare with a lower-capacitance probe before blaming the circuit.

How many degrees of error do two RTD leads add?

Two 1 ohm leads and a local RTD slope of 0.385 ohm/degree C give (1+1)/0.385 = 5.19481 degrees C of indicated error. At 1 mA through a 100 ohm RTD, I squared R is 0.1 mW. A mounting-specific 2 mW/degree C dissipation constant gives 0.05 degrees C self-heating.

What this does not establish

Lead resistance and self-heating are different contributions. Doubling excitation quadruples heating. The local slope is not a complete temperature conversion, and this model does not describe three- or four-wire compensation.

Review check

Measure both leads and record the actual mounting medium and excitation current. Compare settled temperatures at two currents.

What is the thermal noise of a 1 kohm resistor?

At 25 degrees C (298.15 K), sqrt(4 k T R) is 4.05779 nV/sqrt(Hz), using k = 1.380649e-23 J/K. With 10,000 Hz equivalent noise bandwidth, the resistor contributes 0.405779 microvolts RMS. A flat gain of 10 produces 4.05779 microvolts RMS at the output.

What this does not establish

Equivalent noise bandwidth is not automatically the -3 dB bandwidth. This calculation excludes amplifier noise, interference and low-frequency excess noise; it is not a complete circuit noise specification.

Review check

Document the filter noise bandwidth and measurement bandwidth. Compare measured RMS noise after accounting for instrument noise.

Will a bootstrap capacitor hold up during a long on-time?

With 40 nC gate charge, 100 microamps floating supply current, 10 microamps leakage and a 100 microsecond on-time, required charge is 40 + (110 x 100 / 1000) = 51 nC. A 100 nF effective capacitor loses 0.51 V, leaving 0.49 V of a 1 V droop allowance.

What this does not establish

At 1,000 microseconds the same model needs 150 nC and loses 1.5 V, exceeding that allowance. Recharge time, startup, diode drop and driver undervoltage lockout remain separate checks.

Review check

Use effective capacitance after bias and tolerance. Verify the actual driver supply waveform at maximum on-time.

What is the ideal noise floor of a 12-bit ADC?

For a 3.3 V input span and 12 bits, q = 3.3/4096 = 0.805664 mV. Under the uniform quantization-error model, q/sqrt(12) = 232.575 microvolts RMS. A full-span sine has an ideal signal-to-quantization-noise ratio of 74.0081 dB.

What this does not establish

A sine with one-tenth the peak-to-peak amplitude loses 20 dB of that ratio. These are ideal quantities, not measured ENOB or a promise of resolution at DC. Real noise, distortion, reference errors and correlated quantization error can dominate.

Review check

Separate input span, signal amplitude and code width. Compare with measured device performance at the relevant sample rate and bandwidth.

How long does a low-voltage RC node take to discharge?

For a disconnected 5 V node, 10 kohm and 100 microfarads give RC = 1 second. Reaching 0.5 V takes RC ln(5/0.5) = 2.30259 seconds. Initial resistor power is 5 squared / 10,000 = 2.5 mW.

What this does not establish

The exponential model assumes constant resistance and capacitance with no active supply or other current path. It is not a high-voltage discharge procedure or proof that a circuit is safe to touch.

Review check

Check all possible back-power paths and measure the remaining voltage. Include capacitor tolerance and resistor pulse limits.

How much DC current do two RS-485 terminators draw?

Two 120 ohm resistors in parallel make 60 ohms. At 2 V differential voltage, total termination current is 2/60 = 33.3333 mA and total resistor power is 4/60 = 66.6667 mW. Each equal resistor dissipates half the total.

What this does not establish

This is the DC load of the two resistors only. It excludes receivers, failsafe bias and transient behavior; it does not select termination locations or prove signal integrity.

Review check

Record bus topology, actual resistor values and driver differential voltage. Verify driver capability and each resistor rating separately.

How large can a noninverting op-amp DC offset be?

With 90 kohm feedback and 10 kohm to ground, noise gain is 10. A 50 microvolt offset bound contributes 0.5 mV at the output. Independent 20 nA bias-current bounds with a 10 kohm source contribute at most 20 nA x 10 kohm x 10 + 20 nA x 90 kohm = 3.8 mV. The summed bound is 4.3 mV.

What this does not establish

This adds magnitudes conservatively and assumes the amplifier remains linear. It does not assume cancellation between bias currents or include resistor gain error, drift or output saturation.

Review check

Use limits at the operating temperature and common-mode voltage. Keep typical values separate from guaranteed bounds.

Does a 1,000-cycle encoder require a 200 kHz counter?

For 1,000 channel cycles per revolution with X4 decoding, there are 4,000 count events per revolution. At 3,000 rpm, the event rate is 4,000 x 3,000 / 60 = 200,000 events/s. Nominal angle spacing is 360/4,000 = 0.09 degree.

What this does not establish

The channel-cycle frequency is 50 kHz while the decoded event rate is 200 kHz. Manufacturer PPR and CPR labels vary. Angle spacing is not absolute angular accuracy.

Review check

Verify both receiver channel frequency and decoded-counter limits. Test count totals over known revolutions at low and maximum speed.

How often should a 20 ppm clock resynchronize?

A constant 20 ppm error accumulates 20e-6 x 86,400 = 1.728 seconds per day. If the entire remaining drift allowance is 1 second, the maximum drift-only interval is 1/(20e-6 x 3,600) = 13.8889 hours.

What this does not establish

Subtract initial synchronization error and timestamp uncertainty before allocating the drift allowance. Temperature, aging and supply variation must fit the entered frequency-error bound.

Review check

Record the reference clock, elapsed interval and temperature. Review how backward clock corrections affect event ordering.
