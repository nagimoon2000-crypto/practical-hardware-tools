> The calculators are free utilities. The connected Payhip digital product is listed, but no paid sales have been verified. Do not report purchases or buyer interest without evidence.

## Offer: solve a field problem
**Audience:** embedded developers, automation engineers and hardware technicians troubleshooting RS-485/Modbus and adjacent design calculations.

**Free value:** verified interactive calculators and worked examples. Each calculator should help the visitor make one engineering decision, not merely provide a number.

**Paid next step hypothesis:** "RS-485 Troubleshooting Test Pack — a repeatable field checklist and test workflow to identify common wiring, termination, address and timing faults." Confirm exact contents and the live Payhip price before publishing claims or CTAs. No guaranteed repair result, certification, or time saved without measured proof.

**Offer ladder:**
1. Free diagnostic calculator / worksheet.
2. Free RS-485 preview checklist (only when genuinely available).
3. Paid Payhip test pack (existing listing, exact price verified).
4. Team license or expanded engineering pack only after buyer feedback.
Avoid bulk production of new packs until the current pack receives qualified traffic.

## Leads: move visitors to an intentional next step
- Track eligible paths by `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` without collecting sensitive user input.
- Add topic-matched internal links between calculator guides and a genuine related paid solution.
- Publish 3 in-depth problem-first content pieces: (a) RS-485 A/B polarity diagnosis, (b) Modbus RTU timeouts and address conflicts, (c) termination/biasing diagnostic workflow.
- Use engineering forums and GitHub discussions to answer genuine questions first; no spam or fabricated endorsements.
- Do not represent a click to Payhip as a completed order.

## Metrics
`page_view` → `calculator_result` → `paid_product_link_click` → **verified Payhip order**. Record unique visits and meaningful conversion denominator. If sales data is unavailable, record as *unknown*, not zero for unseen periods.

Weekly scorecard: qualified organic visits, tool completion rate, relevant CTA click rate, Payhip product-page visits (where measured), verified checkouts, collected revenue, refunds, creation/support time.
